The Playbook

How It Works

Four rules. Same rules every week. No fuss, no frills — just sales.

01

Mondays Start Fresh

Every Monday at 12:01 AM, the leaderboard resets. Last week’s winners and last week’s losers are even again.

02

Total Sales Win

Stores ranked by total pretzels moved from Monday to Sunday. Singles and doubles all count toward the same total.

03

10% Off The Bill

The #1 store gets 10% off their next supply order. Applied automatically the following Monday morning.

04

7-Eleven Only

Open exclusively to participating 7-Eleven locations in the network. Other channels are tracked separately.
