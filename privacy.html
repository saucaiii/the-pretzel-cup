This page covers the email address you enter on the free-pretzels pack game at the-pretzel-cup.vercel.app/free-pretzels. It is short on purpose. If anything here is unclear, email mcaiazzoppffs@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your email, when you enter it on pack 2 to get the next free pretzel.
  • Whether you tapped the Instagram or share steps (a yes/no flag, no account details).
  • Which voucher codes you earned and whether staff redeemed them in-store.

We do not collect your name, your phone number, your address, or a profile. We do not track you across other sites.

How we use it

  • To email you the magic link that opens the next pack on your phone.
  • To keep one set of packs per person so the offer stays fair.
  • To send you occasional Philly Pretzel Factory news and offers from Philly Pretzel Factory — Lynbrook and Franklin Square. By entering your email and verifying it, you agree to receive these messages. Every email we send has a one-tap way to stop them.

When we delete it

You can ask us to delete your email at any time — reply stop to any email we send, or write to mcaiazzoppffs@gmail.com. We also drop addresses that have gone 24 months without opening an email from us.

Who runs this

Philly Pretzel Factory, Lynbrook and Franklin Square locations, Long Island, New York. Emails come from mcaiazzoppffs@gmail.com. We do not sell or rent your email address. We do not share it with third parties except the services we need to operate (our email provider, our hosting provider, our database provider), each of which is bound by their own privacy terms.

Changes to this page

When this page changes in a meaningful way, we bump the version slug at the top. The slug under which you opted in is recorded against your record, so we always know which version of this page applied to you.

This is a US-only service. Governed by the CAN-SPAM Act and New York state law.
