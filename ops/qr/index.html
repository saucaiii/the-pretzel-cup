How to claim (up to 5 steps)

Look and schedule

Hero colour (solid brand colours only)

After the end date the page says "This offer has ended" and still shows both locations.

Pack game on /free-pretzels

Game on: visitors pick 1 of 5 packs and every pack wins one free pretzel. Then one step at a time: Instagram follow (+1), email (+1), share with their group chat (+1). Max 4 per person; at 3 they can end with "No thanks, I hate delicious and free pretzels." Instagram and share are honour system; Instagram stays hidden until its handle is set, share is always on. A start date is required, and limits reset when it changes. Staff burn codes with the store PIN. Off: the page shows the offer above.

Golden Ticket after all 4

After all 4, players choose: keep their 4 free pretzels, or trade all 4 for the Golden Ticket. Everyone who trades wins it (one fixed item, not a draw). The ticket does not name the prize. At the counter, after the store PIN, staff see what to give plus a surprise: hand back their unused pretzels too (the page never says so). Empty "What staff give" means any one product in store; empty title or line uses the default shown. Needs the Instagram handle.
