Monthly reconciliation

Three-way reconciliation: invoice ↔ Stripe charge ↔ Stripe payout ↔ Chase deposit. Download the CSV, open your Chase statement, and match each payout line to the CSV. Mismatches get flagged in the next ops meeting.
