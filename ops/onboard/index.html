Kit prep

Step 7 of 8

Welcome delivery

Pick the first delivery date and the driver running it. The driver's name and stats will be printed on the driver card and included in the welcome email.

Options come from the people table (driver role).
• Day 1 — call the manager after the first delivery lands
• Week 1 — check sell-through and comfort with the portal
• Month 1 — review volume vs. baseline, adjust standing order
